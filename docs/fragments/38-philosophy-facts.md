### Philosophy
> Learning how to learn beats learning what to learn — master the method once, and every subject after it gets easier.
<details><summary>Random Facts</summary>

- 🫖 **Straight from the kettle** - I don't use a glass; I drink directly from the kettle. It speeds up my hydration bureaucracy, so I never feel dehydrated.
- 🥚 **Boiled and steamed food fan** - boiled vegetables, boiled eggs, boiled tofu. My absolute favorite is tahu pepes (steamed seasoned tofu).
- 🧭 **Self-directed learner** - I learn best on my own: it's faster, and I control both the pace and the difficulty of what I study. Freedom of learning!
